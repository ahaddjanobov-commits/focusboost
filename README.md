# FocusBoost

A free, student-friendly study timer and focus tracker powered by the Pomodoro Technique.

## What is FocusBoost?

FocusBoost helps middle school students (and anyone else) study smarter by automatically breaking study sessions into focused work intervals and short/long breaks. The app tracks your Focus Score in real time, rewards you for staying on task, and logs distractions so you can see exactly where you're losing focus.

## Features

- **Customizable Timer** — Set study time from 15 minutes up to 10 hours
- **Pomodoro Technique** — Work and break cycles automatically scale to your session length
- **Focus Score Tracking** — Earn +1 point every 10 seconds of focused work; lose 5 points when distracted
- **Session History** — Track all your study sessions throughout the day
- **Best Score Tracker** — See your personal record and aim to beat it
- **Smart Feedback** — Performance messages that encourage improvement
- **Audio Alert** — Pleasant chime when your timer ends

## How to Use

1. Open the app in your browser
2. Set your desired study time (or use a quick preset)
3. Hit **Start Study Session**
4. Study while the timer runs
5. Click **I Got Distracted** if you lose focus
6. View your results when the timer ends
7. Track your progress throughout the day

## How Pomodoro Works in FocusBoost

The work and break lengths automatically adjust based on your total session duration:

- **15–30 min** → 15m work / 3m short break / 10m long break
- **30–60 min** → 25m work / 5m short break / 15m long break
- **1–2 hrs** → 30m work / 5m short break / 15m long break
- **2–4 hrs** → 45m work / 10m short break / 20m long break
- **4+ hrs** → 50m work / 10m short break / 25m long break

Every 4 pomodoros, you get a long break instead of a short one.
